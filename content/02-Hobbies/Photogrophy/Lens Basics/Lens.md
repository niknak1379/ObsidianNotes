# Lens

## Focal Length(mm number):
basically the zoom of the camera and how wide angle the shot looks. 
## Aperture(the f number):
(the circle thing that opens and closes in the camera that decides how much light you let in to the sensor)
So this is important for low light
#### Also decides depth of field:
the lower the F number the blurier the background is
